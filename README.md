# 🍵 Tea Theory

**Discover the art of tea-making in Minecraft!**

**Tea Theory** adds a more immersive and realistic approach to brewing tea, introducing naturally generating **Camellia Sinensis** bushes, harvestable tea leaves, processing stages, and a variety of traditional teas — each with its own unique effects.

Rather than simply crafting a finished drink from a few ingredients, Tea Theory gives tea-making a **multi-step process** inspired by real-world tea production. Harvest, process, roast, roll, age, and prepare your leaves before finally brewing them into a drink.

## 🌿 How to Make Tea

Start by finding naturally generated **Camellia Sinensis bushes** and harvesting their leaves. From there, the leaves can be processed in different ways to create a variety of teas.

Tea production uses a combination of the **custom Tea Boiler** and familiar vanilla Minecraft stations, making the process feel integrated with the vanilla crafting system while adding additional depth.

### 🍃 Tea Varieties

*   **Sencha** — Process **Camellia Sinensis Leaves** in the Tea Boiler.
*   **Fukamushicha Sencha** — Process **Sencha Leaves** further in the boiler for a deeper level of processing.
*   **Hojicha** — Roast **Sencha Leaves** using a blasting recipe.
*   **Matcha** — Use a **Stonecutter** to grind Camellia Sinensis into **Matcha Powder**.
*   **Silver Needle** — Cook the rare **Leaf Bud** over a campfire to produce **Silver Needle Tea Leaves**.
*   **Oolong & Black Tea** — Roll Camellia Sinensis Leaves using a reusable **Rolling Pin**. The leaves gradually develop while they remain in your inventory, first becoming **Oolong Tea Leaves** and eventually **Black Tea Leaves**.
*   **Black Tea Milk** — Combine **Black Tea** with a **Milk Bucket** to create a creamy tea drink.

## 🫖 A More Realistic Tea-Making Process

Tea Theory focuses on making tea feel like an actual **process rather than a single crafting recipe**.

Different teas are produced through different combinations of **processing, heating, rolling, and gradual aging**, reflecting how tea leaves are treated in real-world tea production. Some varieties require additional processing, while others rely on roasting or allowing the leaves to change over time.

This means that the tea you make depends on **how you process the leaves**, giving each variety its own identity and making tea production feel like a small craft within Minecraft.

## ✨ Tea Effects

Once prepared, each tea provides its own effects. Depending on the variety, teas can grant useful abilities such as:

*   ⚡ **Speed**
*   ⛏️ **Haste**
*   🔥 **Fire Resistance**
*   🪶 **Slow Falling**
*   And more!

**Hold Shift while hovering over a tea** to view its description and see the effects it provides.

## ⚙️ How It Works

Tea Theory combines custom mechanics with familiar Minecraft systems:

*   **Tea Boiler** — A custom processing block and recipe type for preparing tea leaves.
*   **Vanilla Stations** — Uses campfires, blast furnaces/blasting recipes, stonecutters, and crafting alongside the custom boiler.
*   **Leaf Aging/Oxidisation** — Rolled leaves gradually transform into different tea varieties while in your inventory.
*   **Bush Harvesting** — Naturally generated Camellia Sinensis bushes provide the starting materials for tea production, found in forest biomes.
*   **Custom Effects** — Finished teas provide their own unique effects.
*   **Informative Tooltips** — Hold **Shift** to view additional tea information and effects.
